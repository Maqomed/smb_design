01 — Color Palettes
Choose your palette

Three professional options for an ERP interface. All palettes use the same semantic token structure — swapping is instant.

1 — Arctic
✓
Icy, crisp, Scandinavian. Deep polar blue sidebar, muted ice-blue accent. Cold and precise — like "Winter Chill".
2 — Nimbus
✓
Storm-gray-blue sidebar, warm amber accent like morning sun cutting through clouds. Dramatic contrast, memorable identity.
3 — Corporate Navy
✓
Classic deep navy sidebar, standard business blue accent. The most traditional enterprise ERP look — IBM, HP, Oracle style. Universally trusted.
4 — Charcoal Steel
✓
Dark charcoal sidebar with a restrained steel-blue accent on neutral gray. Zero decoration, all function. Financial data terminals, accounting software, Bloomberg-style.
5 — Deep Teal
✓
Deep teal sidebar, muted teal accent. Conservative and professional — used widely in banking, insurance, and healthcare ERP systems. Calm, trustworthy, serious.
